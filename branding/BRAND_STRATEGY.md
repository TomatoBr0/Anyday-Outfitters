# Brand strategy & market review

## Market snapshot (October 2026)

| App | Strength | Gap Getup exploits |
|---|---|---|
| **Whering** | Free, unlimited items, sustainability angle, moodboards | Little automatic outfit generation; you do the styling |
| **Indyx** | Deep closet tools, cost-per-wear, resale, human stylists | Reviewers call the UI dated and complex |
| **Acloset** | Popular AI closet, weather-based suggestions | Heavy setup; suggestions can feel repetitive |
| **Cladwell** | Capsule wardrobe and weather-based outfits from what you own | Subscription-first; older audience and tone |
| **Fits / Alta** | AI stylist, virtual try-on, social and community | Shopping and social pressure; cloud AI; privacy trade-offs |
| **Stylebook** | Long-running, powerful | Paid upfront; manual; dated |

*Sources: competitor sites and app listings, getwardrobe.com/compare, fits-app.com, a Medium review roundup (2026). Most comparisons are written by vendors, so treat claims as directional.*

**Pattern.** The category splits between *catalog tools* (great closets, you style) and *AI stylists* (cloud AI, shopping, social). Nobody owns **"fast, private, morning-first"**: one tap, the right outfit for today's weather and plans, from your own clothes, explained.

## Audience

Primary: **16–26-year-olds** (high school, college, early career) who care how they look, own more than they wear, and are short on morning time. They mix aesthetics (streetwear on Monday, smart-casual for a presentation), and the app is gender-neutral by design.

Jobs to be done:
1. "Tell me what to wear *right now* so I'm not late."
2. "Help me use the clothes I forgot I had."
3. "Make sure I don't show up underdressed or soaked."
4. "Help me pack without overpacking."

## Positioning

> **For** people who want to look good without spending the morning on it, **Getup** is the outfit planner that **dresses you from your own closet in one tap**, accounting for weather, plans and laundry. **Unlike** stylist apps that push shopping or need cloud AI, Getup works offline, keeps your photos on your phone, and explains every pick.

## Differentiators (built, not promised)

1. **Morning-first home screen.** Weather, today's event and a complete outfit with a "Wear today" button, all on the first screen.
2. **Explainable picks.** "Rain-ready outer layer · Footwear handles wet ground · Brings back your cream sweater."
3. **Laundry reality.** Per-item wash rules mean jeans aren't "dirty" after one wear.
4. **Private by architecture.** No account and no server; data is on-device.
5. **Free where it counts.** The full daily loop is free.

## Growth hypotheses to test

- **TikTok / Reels "closet clean-out" format**: "I let an app dress me for a week."
- **Campus ambassadors** at the start of the semester (packing lists for move-in).
- **Shareable outfit cards** (planned): a styled image of today's fit with a subtle "via Getup".
- **App Store search terms** in priority order: *outfit planner, what to wear, closet organizer, wardrobe app, outfit ideas, packing list*.
