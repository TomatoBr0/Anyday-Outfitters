# Naming research

**Recommendation: Getup.** Runner-up: **Kitted**.
*Checked 2026-10-21. Domain checks are RDAP lookups (a 404 means not found in the registry, which suggests it's unregistered but doesn't guarantee it). App Store checks used Apple's public search API. Trademark notes come from public aggregators (Justia/TrademarkDir) and are **not** a clearance search.*

## The brief, distilled

The name should be short, memorable and fashion-relevant without saying "AI". It should feel natural to teens and young adults, work as an icon with no text, and carry the promise: *open the app, get dressed, get on with your day.*

## Why Getup

- **The double meaning is the product.** You *get up*, and Getup hands you your *getup*. The tagline writes itself: **"Get up. Get dressed. Get going."**
- **It's ownable visually.** The mark is a hanger whose bar is a horizon with the sun rising through it. It works at 29 px with no text.
- **The tone is right.** It's casual, a little playful, and gender-neutral. It doesn't sound like an AI startup or a discount store.
- **The risks are known.** First, the Class 25 "GET UP" registration: a likelihood-of-confusion analysis is needed before filing. Second, a crowded "GetUp" alarm-app namespace, which the App Store name **"Getup: Outfit Planner"** sidesteps.

**If legal review blocks Getup, switch to Kitted.** The brand system (palette, type, hanger-sunrise mark) still works without the "get up" wordplay. Renaming takes about 10 minutes: see `docs/RENAMING.md`.

## Addendum: does the name still work with social? (2026-10-21)

The product now combines a wardrobe assistant, outfit recommendations, inspiration, outfit sharing and recreating looks. I re-scored the finalists for the community side:

| | Getup | Kitted | Tuck |
|---|---|---|---|
| Works as a verb or noun people say when posting | ✅ "Post your getup", "#getup", "today's getup" | ✅ "kitted out" | ⚠️ awkward |
| Sounds like a community, not a retailer | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Still fits a private AI wardrobe | ✅ | ✅ | ⚠️ |
| Handle crowding (GetUp alarm apps, GET UP apparel mark) | ⚠️ unchanged | ✅ | ✅ |

**Decision: keep Getup.** "Getup" is literally the word for the thing people share, so social strengthens the name. The trademark caveat is unchanged; Kitted remains the fallback.

Updated taglines:
- Primary (unchanged): **Get up. Get dressed. Get going.**
- Social / recreate line: **See it. Recreate it. From your own closet.**
- Short: **Your closet, your style, your crew.**
