**Description:**

Getup turns the clothes you already own into a ready-to-wear outfit every morning.

Open the app and your look is already there, built from your own closet for today's weather, your plans and what's actually clean. Tap "Wear today" and get on with your day.

YOUR CLOSET, ORGANIZED
• Snap your clothes or pick several photos at once. Getup suggests the color for you.
• Rotate and crop photos, then add only the details you care about.
• Search and filter by category, color, style, favorites or what's clean.
• Not ready to photograph everything? Start with a sample closet.

YOUR STYLE, UNDERSTOOD
• Choose any mix of styles: streetwear, minimalist, preppy, smart casual, athletic, vintage, workwear and more.
• Tell Getup if you run warm or cold. Two people dress differently at 65°.
• Like or pass on outfits and your suggestions adapt.
• Every pick tells you why: "Rain-ready outer layer · Brings back your cream sweater."

YOUR OUTFITS, READY
• A complete outfit every morning, plus alternatives.
• Lock a favorite piece and Getup styles everything else around it.
• Swap any piece with ranked alternatives.
• Plan outfits for interviews, dates and events ahead of time.
• Build packing lists for trips from your own closet, using the destination forecast.

LAUNDRY THAT MATCHES REAL LIFE
• Set how many wears each piece lasts: a tee after one, jeans after five, a coat after twenty.
• Clear the hamper in one tap. Dirty clothes are never suggested.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. No ad trackers.
• Your photos, outfits and history stay on your phone.
• Works offline, and falls back gracefully when there's no forecast.

Free to use: every feature you need to get dressed each day is included.

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com.
