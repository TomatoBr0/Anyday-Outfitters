# Getup Privacy Policy — DRAFT

> **Draft for legal review. Not legal advice and not yet in force.** Bracketed items need decisions. This draft describes the MVP as built: on-device storage, no accounts, no analytics.

_Last updated: [date]_

Getup ("we", "the app") helps you organize your clothes and plan outfits. We built it so your wardrobe stays yours.

## What we collect

**Nothing is sent to us.** Getup has no accounts, no servers that receive your data, no advertising and no analytics SDKs.

The data you create stays **on your device**:

| Data | Where it lives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Clothing photos | The app's private storage on your phone | Showing your closet |
| Clothing details, outfits, wear history, laundry status | The app's private storage | Making recommendations |
| Events and trips you add | The app's private storage | Planning outfits |
| Preferences (styles, fit, temperature comfort) | The app's private storage | Personalizing suggestions |
| Location (if you allow it), rounded to about 1 km | The app's private storage | Local weather |

## Third parties

- **Weather and city search: Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com). To get a forecast, the app sends your approximate coordinates (rounded to about 1 km) or the city name you type. No identifier is sent. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to those requests. [Update if the provider changes.]
- **Apple / Google** provide the operating system, app store and photo picker. Their policies apply.

## Permissions

- **Camera / Photos:** used only when you choose to add clothing. Photos you pick are copied into the app; your library is never scanned.
- **Location (while using the app):** used only to fetch weather, and only if you tap "Use my current location". You can enter a city instead.

You can change these in iOS Settings at any time.

## Your choices

- **Export:** Profile → Privacy & data → Export (JSON of your item details, outfits and history).
- **Delete:** Profile → Privacy & data → Delete all data permanently erases everything on the device. Deleting the app does too.

## Children

Getup is designed for teens and adults. [Decide: minimum age 13 in the US; consider 16 in some EU states.] We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and since no data leaves the device, we don't hold any.

## Future features

If we add optional cloud sync, AI styling or payments, we'll update this policy **before** they launch, and they will be opt-in.
