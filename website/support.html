Getting started

Add five or six pieces you wear often — a couple of tops, a bottom or two and a pair of shoes. Today’s outfit appears instantly.

Outfits feel repetitive

Tap the refresh icon for new options, use “Not my style” on looks you don’t like, and add a few more pieces. Getup avoids repeating items you wore in the last few days (adjust in Profile → Style & fit).

Something I wore is still showing

Items go to the hamper based on their own wash rule. Mark them dirty from the item page, or change rules in Profile → Laundry rules.

Weather isn’t showing

Add your city in Profile → Weather & location. Offline, Getup uses the last forecast it saw, or a clearly labeled seasonal estimate.
