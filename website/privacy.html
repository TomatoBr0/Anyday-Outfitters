Privacy

Draft policy for the pre-release MVP, pending legal review.

Getup is built so your wardrobe stays yours. There are no accounts, no ad trackers and no analytics SDKs in the app.

What stays on your phone

Clothing photos, item details, outfits, wear history, laundry status, events, trips and preferences are stored only on your device. We never receive them.

What leaves your phone

To show weather, the app sends your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) or the city name you type to our weather provider, Open-Meteo. No identifier is attached.

Permissions

  • Camera & Photos — only when you add clothing. Your library is never scanned.
  • Location (while using) — only when you tap “Use my current location”. You can enter a city instead.

Your control

Export your data or delete everything from Profile → Privacy & data. Deleting the app also removes it all.

Website

If you join the waitlist, we store your email address only to invite you to the beta. Email us to have it removed.
