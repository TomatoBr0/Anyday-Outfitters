How it works

  1. 1

    Snap your clothes

    Photograph a few favorites — or start with a sample closet. Getup suggests the color from your photo; you confirm.

  2. 2

    Open the app

    Getup checks your forecast, today’s plans and your laundry, then builds a complete look from what you own.

  3. 3

    Get dressed

    Tap Wear today. Your wear history updates, laundry is tracked, and tomorrow’s pick stays fresh.

Why the hanger has a sunrise

Getup started with one small frustration: standing in front of a full closet, already late. The name says the whole idea twice — you get up, and we hand you your getup. The mark is a hanger whose bar is a horizon, with the sun coming up through it. Morning, handled.

Your closet stays yours

No account. No ad trackers. Your photos, outfits and history live on your phone. The only thing that leaves is your approximate location — to fetch the forecast.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Everything you need to get dressed every day — closet, daily outfits, laundry, planning, packing — is free. A future “Plus” tier may add extras like sync and advanced insights.

Do I have to photograph my entire closet?

No. Five or six pieces is enough for your first outfit. Add the rest whenever — you can even add items without a photo.

Is this “AI”?

It’s a transparent scoring engine — style, color, weather, occasion, your feedback, and rotation — that runs entirely on your phone. Every suggestion tells you why it was chosen.

Does it work for any style?

Streetwear, minimalist, preppy, workwear, athletic, vintage, formal — pick several. Getup never assumes what you should wear based on gender.

Android?

iPhone first. Android is built from the same code and will follow.
